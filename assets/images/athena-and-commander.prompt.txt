Draw a wide landscape illustration (about 3:2), showing two characters
standing side by side, in loose pencil sketch style.

On the left, Athena: a cute 2.5-head-tall SD chibi girl with shoulder-length
wavy hair and a small Corinthian helmet worn like a hat, tilted back so her
whole face is visible. Large round curious eyes. She wears a short sleeveless
Greek chiton dress with a shoulder brooch and a waist cord, and simple
sandals. A palm-sized round owl is perched on her shoulder. She holds up a
single sheet of paper toward her companion, looking up at him with her head
tilted, asking something. The writing on the paper is drawn as wavy squiggle
lines, not readable letters.

On the right, the Commander: a cute 2.5-head-tall SD chibi young man in the
same proportions and style, standing barely taller than her — their heads at
almost the same level. Clean-shaven and youthful, clearly slim and lightly
built with narrow shoulders and thin limbs — only his face is round, with
soft full cheeks. Short hair parted 6:4 to one side with softly flicked ends.
Round thin metal-rimmed glasses, the frame drawn in plain grey line and left
uncolored. Large gentle eyes with slightly downturned outer corners and an
easy warm smile. He wears a neat fitted cleric robe with a broad collar and a
waist cord, and a short stole with tasseled ends and an embroidered hem
border. At his collar he wears only a plain small round medallion — no cross,
and no religious symbol of any kind anywhere on him or his clothing. In his
far hand, on the outer edge of the frame, he holds a tall ornate wooden staff
crowned with an armillary sphere — interlocking metal rings around a small
central orb. He listens to her with a warm smile, nodding slightly.

Both characters are shown full body, standing close together and facing
slightly toward each other, with generous empty space above and around them.
Make the background fully transparent if possible; otherwise use a plain flat
white background. No scenery and no ground shadow.

Style: loose pencil sketch, monochrome graphite line art with light hatching.
The entire drawing is grayscale EXCEPT two accent colors, kept strictly
separate. Warm gold (#c9a227) appears ONLY on Athena's helmet and shoulder
brooch. Warm vermilion (#b8412e) appears ONLY on the Commander's stole trim,
his tasseled cord, and the central orb of his staff. Never put gold on the
Commander or vermilion on Athena. Everything else, including both characters'
hair and his glasses, stays grey. No writing or lettering anywhere.
