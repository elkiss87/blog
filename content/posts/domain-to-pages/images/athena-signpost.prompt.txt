Draw a wide landscape illustration (about 3:2), in loose pencil sketch style.

A cute 2.5-head-tall SD chibi girl named Athena: shoulder-length wavy hair and
a small Corinthian helmet worn like a hat, tilted back so her whole face is
visible. Large round eyes. She wears a short sleeveless Greek chiton dress
with a shoulder brooch and a waist cord, and simple sandals. A palm-sized
round owl is perched on her shoulder.

She stands at the foot of a tall wooden signpost, leaning back on her heels
and looking straight up at it with wide round eyes and her mouth slightly
open. The post carries four arrow-shaped boards mounted at different heights,
each pointing a different way, and from the widest board three much smaller
plaques hang in a row on short chains. Every board and plaque is covered edge
to edge with wavy squiggle lines instead of words.

Frame it from low down and looking upward, so the signpost rises tall through
the upper part of the frame and she stands small at its foot. Generous empty
space around them and no other scenery at all — no wall, no building, just the
one post. Make the background fully transparent if possible; otherwise use a
plain flat white background. No ground shadow.

Style: loose pencil sketch, monochrome graphite line art with light hatching.
The entire drawing is grayscale EXCEPT one warm gold accent (#c9a227), used
only on three things — her helmet, her shoulder brooch, and the single widest
board that the small plaques hang from. Everything else, including her hair,
the owl, the post, the other boards, and the small hanging plaques, stays
grey. No writing or lettering anywhere in the image.
