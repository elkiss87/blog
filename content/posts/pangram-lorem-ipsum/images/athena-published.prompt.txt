space around them, and there is no other scenery at all. Make the background
fully transparent if possible; otherwise use a plain flat white background. No

Style: loose pencil sketch, monochrome graphite line art with light hatching.
The entire drawing is grayscale EXCEPT one warm gold accent (#c9a227), used
