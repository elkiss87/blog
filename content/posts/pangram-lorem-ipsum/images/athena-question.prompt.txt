Draw a wide landscape illustration (about 3:2) of a single character, in loose
pencil sketch style.

A cute 2.5-head-tall SD chibi girl named Athena: shoulder-length wavy hair and
a small Corinthian helmet worn like a hat, tilted back so her whole face is
visible. Large round curious eyes. She wears a short sleeveless Greek chiton
dress with a shoulder brooch and a waist cord, and simple sandals. A
palm-sized round owl is perched on her shoulder.

She holds three sheets of paper fanned out in one hand and looks up at them
with wide curious eyes, her head tilted to one side. The writing on the papers
is drawn as wavy squiggle lines, not readable letters. The owl tilts its head
in the same direction as hers. A small question mark floats above her head.

She stands centered in the frame with generous empty space around her. Make
the background fully transparent if possible; otherwise use a plain flat white
background. No scenery and no ground shadow.

Style: loose pencil sketch, monochrome graphite line art with light hatching.
The entire drawing is grayscale EXCEPT one warm gold accent (#c9a227), used
only on three things — her helmet, her shoulder brooch, and the floating
question mark. Everything else, including her hair and the owl, stays grey.
No writing or lettering anywhere in the image.
